Oysters with Ponzu Mignonette

Ice-cold freshly shucked oysters dressed with a bright Japanese mignonette of rice wine vinegar, soy sauce, lemon, toasted sesame oil, shallot, and green onion.

Prep

15 mins

Total

45 mins

Yield

8–12 oysters

Ingredients

Tap to check off
Batch Scale
Oysters & Ponzu Mignonette

Instructions

Tap a step when done
Mignonette & Service

1. Make the Mignonette First: Whisk the rice wine vinegar, soy sauce, fresh lemon juice, sugar, and sesame oil until the sugar completely dissolves.

2. Mellow the Aromatics: Stir in the finely minced shallot and sliced green onion. Refrigerate at least 20–30 minutes (or several hours ahead) so the shallots mellow in the citrus-soy liquid.

3. Shuck & Serve: Shuck the oysters clean of any shell grit, nestle level on a platter of crushed ice or rock salt, and spoon ~1/2 teaspoon of chilled ponzu mignonette over each oyster.
