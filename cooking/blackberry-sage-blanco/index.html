Blackberry Sage Blanco Batch

Crowd-pleasing 20-drink blanco tequila pitcher infused with blended fresh blackberries, 25 aromatic sage leaves, fresh lime juice, and agave.

Prep

15 mins

Total

3 hours

Yield

20 drinks

Ingredients

Tap to check off
Batch Scale
20-Drink Party Batch

Instructions

Tap a step when done
Blend, Strain & Marry

1. Blend the Base: Combine the 5 cups blackberries, fresh lime juice, agave/simple syrup sweetener, and all 25 sage leaves in a blender and blend on high until liquefied.

2. Fine-Strain: Pour through a fine-mesh strainer directly into a large pitcher, pressing with a spoon to extract all ruby juice while catching seeds and herb bits.

3. Batch & Chill: Stir in the 5 cups blanco tequila, cover tightly, and refrigerate for a few hours so the earthy sage and berry flavors marry.

4. Serve: Stir vigorously right before pouring over fresh rocks. Garnish each glass with two skewered blackberries and a single fresh sage leaf resting on the ice.
