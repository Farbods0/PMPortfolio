Signature Chicken Marinades (7 Flavors)

High-flavor bulk meal-prep marinades from Korean Gochujang and Yakitori to Cantonese Char Siu, Vietnamese Lemongrass, Peruvian Pollo a la Brasa, and Fiery Harissa.

Prep

15 mins

Total

8–24 hours

Yield

7 Bulk Marinades

Ingredients

Tap to check off
Batch Scale
1. Gochujang Chicken (6–8 Hrs Max · 16–20 Thighs)
2. Yakitori & 3. Teriyaki Marinades (12 Hrs)
4. Macro Cantonese Char Siu (12–24 Hrs · 10 lbs Breast)
5. Vietnamese Lemongrass & Garlic (12–24 Hrs · 10 lbs)
6. Peruvian Pollo a la Brasa (12–24 Hrs · 10 lbs)
7. Fiery Harissa & Toasted Spice (8–24 Hrs · 10 lbs)

Instructions

Tap a step when done
Marinating & High-Heat Char

1. Whisk Smooth: Whisk all ingredients for your chosen marinade in a large bowl until fully emulsified (if making Cantonese Char Siu, whisk the xanthan gum in last so it thickens into a clingy glaze without clumping).

2. Cure & Marinate: Toss chicken thighs or evenly butterflied/pounded chicken breasts in heavy-duty zip-top bags or Cambro containers, massaging to coat every surface. Respect the window: 6–8 hours max for Gochujang so it stays tender, and 12–24 hours for the 10-lb macro marinades.

3. Grill, Broil, or Air-Fry: Cook over a medium-high oiled grill, under the oven broiler, or in a 400°F air fryer, flipping until edges blister and caramelize and internal temperature hits 160°F–165°F for breast (175°F for thighs). Rest 5–10 minutes before slicing against the grain.
