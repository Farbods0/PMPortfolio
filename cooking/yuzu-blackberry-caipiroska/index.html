Yuzu Blackberry Caipiroska

Nobu-inspired cocktail of muddled ripe blackberries, floral Japanese yuzu juice, simple syrup, and chilled vodka poured unstrained over crushed rocks with mint and dried citrus.

Prep Time

5 mins

Total Time

5 mins

Yield

1 cocktail

INGREDIENTS

Tap to check off
Per Cocktail (Made Fresh to Order)

INSTRUCTIONS

Tap a step to mark done
Muddle, Shake & Pour

1. Muddle: In a cocktail shaker, muddle the fresh blackberries with simple syrup until completely broken down and juicy.

2. Shake Hard: Add the vodka and yuzu juice, fill with ice, and shake hard for 10–15 seconds.

3. Dirty Dump & Garnish: Pour the entire contents — ice, crushed berry pulp, and all — straight into a rocks glass. Slap the mint sprig between your palms to release its oils and garnish alongside a dried citrus wheel.
