Ninja Creami Oreo McFlurry

Ultra-thick vanilla bean high-protein soft-serve spun in the Ninja Creami with chunky Oreo Thins folded throughout — 281 calories and 23g protein for the entire pint.

Prep Time

5 mins

Total Time

16 hours

Yield

1 pint (281 cal · 23g P)

INGREDIENTS

Tap to check off
Ice Cream Base (281 cal · 31g C · 6g F · 23g P)
Mix-Ins

INSTRUCTIONS

Tap a step to mark done
Freeze, Spin & Mix-In

1. Blend all base ingredients until completely smooth, pour into a Ninja Creami pint, and freeze flat for at least 16 hours.

2. Run the outside of the frozen pint under hot water for 60 seconds.

3. Spin on the Lite Ice Cream setting, then run a Respin cycle (respin once more if still slightly powdery).

4. Carve a tunnel down the center of the pint with a butter knife, pack in the 3 Oreo Thins, and run the Mix-In cycle.
