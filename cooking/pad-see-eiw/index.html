Entree

Beef Pad See Ew

Wok-charred wide rice noodles tossed with soy-marinated flank steak, crisp-tender Chinese broccoli, scrambled eggs, and a caramelized dark soy and oyster sauce glaze.

Prep

20 mins

Total

35 mins

Yield

6–8 servings

Ingredients

Tap to check off
Batch Scale
Steak, Noodles & Greens
Marinade & Stir-Fry Glaze

Instructions

Tap a step when done
Prep & High-Heat Wok Stir-Fry

1. Toss the thinly sliced flank steak with light soy sauce in a bowl and marinate while prepping the remaining ingredients.

2. Boil or soak the wide rice noodles just until tender-pliable (2–3 minutes for fresh), drain thoroughly, and toss with a drizzle of oil so they don't stick together.

3. Whisk together the dark soy sauce, remaining light soy sauce, minced garlic, oyster sauce, rice vinegar, brown sugar, and MSG until the sugar dissolves.

4. Heat oil in a smoking-hot wok over medium-high to high heat. Sear the beef in batches for 2–3 minutes until browned, toss in the Chinese broccoli for 2 minutes until crisp-tender, and transfer to a bowl.

5. Add remaining oil to the wok, crack in the eggs, and scramble for ~1 minute. Add the noodles and sauce, folding gently so the dark soy caramelizes onto the wide noodles, then return the steak and Chinese broccoli for 2 minutes with a dash of fish sauce.

6. Plate hot and finish with thinly sliced scallions and toasted sesame seeds.
