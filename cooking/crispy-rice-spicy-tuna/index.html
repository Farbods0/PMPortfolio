Crispy Rice with Spicy Tuna

Nobu-style bite-sized blocks of chilled sushi rice deep-fried until shattering and golden, crowned with finely diced sashimi-grade ahi tuna in Kewpie-sriracha dressing.

Prep Time

25 mins

Total Time

8 hours

Yield

16–20 bites

INGREDIENTS

Tap to check off
Crispy Sushi Rice
Spicy Tuna Mixture

INSTRUCTIONS

Tap a step to mark done
Prep, Fry & Assemble

1. Press Rice Overnight: Pack warm seasoned sushi rice firmly into a plastic-lined rectangular pan (~3/4-inch thick), cover, and chill overnight until completely firm.

2. Mix the Spicy Tuna: With a sharp knife on a cold cutting board, dice the sushi-grade ahi tuna very finely — uniform tiny cubes with texture, not a paste. Gently fold in Kewpie mayonnaise, sriracha, sesame oil, and soy sauce. Cover and keep chilled.

3. Cut & Fry Crispy Rice: Slice the cold rice block into bite-sized rectangles using a wet sharp knife so it doesn't stick. Heat oil to 350°F and deep-fry in batches for 2–3 minutes until crisp and golden brown on all sides.

4. Drain & Top: Drain crispy rice on a wire rack (holds crisp for 30–45 minutes), then spoon a generous mound of chilled spicy tuna onto each warm cake right before serving.
