Nobu Miso Black Cod

Silky, buttery black cod marinated for 48 hours in sweet Saikyo miso and broiled on high until the edges blister into Nobu's signature mahogany caramelization.

Prep

15 mins

Total

48 hours

Yield

4 fillets

Ingredients

Tap to check off
Fish & Marinade

Instructions

Tap a step when done
48-Hour Miso Cure & High-Heat Broil

1. Make the Saikyo Miso Marinade: Combine sake and mirin in a small saucepan and bring to a boil for 20 seconds to cook off the alcohol. Turn heat to low, whisk in the white miso paste until dissolved, then whisk in the sugar until glossy and smooth. Cool completely to room temperature.

2. Marinate 48 Hours: Pat the black cod fillets thoroughly dry with paper towels, coat generously in the cooled miso marinade inside a glass dish or zip-top bag, and refrigerate for 48 hours.

3. Prep to Broil: Preheat the oven broiler to HIGH and line a rimmed sheet pan with foil. Gently wipe off excess marinade from each fillet with a paper towel — do not rinse, leaving a thin glaze on the fish.

4. Broil: Arrange fillets skin-side down on the foil-lined pan and broil on high for 8–12 minutes, watching closely after minute 6.

5. Check for Signature Char: Look for deep, dark golden-brown caramelization blistering along the edges while the center turns opaque and flakes effortlessly with a fork. Plate immediately.
