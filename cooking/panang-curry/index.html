Chicken Panang Curry

Velvety Thai red coconut curry enriched with creamy peanut butter, tender sliced chicken, sweet bell peppers, fresh ginger, lime juice, and fragrant basil over hot jasmine rice.

Prep

15 mins

Total

35 mins

Yield

8 servings

Ingredients

Tap to check off
Batch Scale
Panang Curry

Instructions

Tap a step when done
Panang Curry

1. Heat oil over medium-high heat in a large skillet or Dutch oven. Sauté sliced onions for 2 minutes, then add bell peppers, minced garlic, and grated ginger and sauté 2 minutes more until fragrant.

2. Stir in the Panang curry paste and peanut butter and fry for 1 minute to bloom the spices. Pour in most of the coconut milk, whisk the cornstarch smoothly into the remaining 1/2 can of coconut milk, and stir it into the pot.

3. Add the sliced chicken, stirring to coat. Simmer gently for 10-15 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the coconut-peanut sauce thickens.

4. Stir in brown sugar, fish sauce, fresh lime juice, and chopped basil. Simmer 5 minutes more and adjust salt and pepper to taste.

5. Ladle generously over steaming hot jasmine rice.
