Biang Biang Braised Pork Noodles

Interactive dinner-party hand-pulled wide belt noodles topped with 3.5-hour star-anise braised pork, sizzling 350°F garlic-chili oil, spicy cucumber salad, and blistered shishitos.

Prep

1 hour

Total

5.5 hours

Yield

12 servings

Ingredients

Tap to check off
Oven-Braised Pork
Triple Hand-Pulled Noodle Dough (38–40 Pieces)
Bowl Base, Hot-Oil Sizzle & Greens
Spicy Cucumber Salad & Garlic Shishitos

Instructions

Tap a step when done
Braised Pork & Resting the Belt-Noodle Dough

1. Prep Aromatics: Mince 30 garlic cloves for the noodle station, slice 10 cloves into thin chips for shishitos, and sliver 6 cloves for spinach. Slice chillies and scallions.

2. Oven-Braise the Pork (3.5 Hours): Combine pork stock, Shaoxing wine, soy sauce, star anise, ginger, bruised garlic, pork belly chunks, and pork shoulder in a heavy Dutch oven. Cover and braise in a 300°F (150°C) oven for 3.5 hours until fork-tender, then shred and moisten with braising jus.

3. Mix & Knead Noodle Dough: Mix 1.4 kg flour, 20g kosher salt, and 700g water into a shaggy dough. Wrap in plastic and rest 20 minutes, then knead 5 minutes until smooth.

4. Portion & Oil Heavily: Roll dough into logs and cut into 38–40 pieces (~50g each). Flatten each into a 5–6 inch rectangle, coat every surface generously in oil so they never skin over, layer on a tray, wrap tightly in plastic, and rest at room temperature (at least 1–2 hours for effortless stretching).

Sides & The Interactive Hot-Oil Noodle Sizzle

1. Chilled Spicy Cucumbers: Toss spiralized cucumbers with 4 tsp salt, sit 5 minutes, rinse 3 times, and drain well. Toss with soy sauce, minced garlic, rice vinegar, chili oil, sugar, sesame oil, and sesame seeds; chill immediately.

2. Spinach & Blistered Shishitos: Sauté the 6 slivered garlic cloves with 14 oz spinach and set aside. Blister shishitos in a smoking-hot wok with oil and salt; crisp the 10 garlic chips, then toss peppers back in with 2.5 tsp sesame oil and 2.5 tsp soy sauce.

3. Set the Bowl Station: Line up 12 bowls, each dosed with 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp black vinegar, a pinch of sugar, and a pinch of MSG. Set out bowls of chili flakes, Doubanjiang, minced garlic, scallions, and sesame seeds.

4. Pull, Boil & Sizzle: Bring two large pots of water to a rolling boil and heat 1 cup lard or neutral oil to 350°F. Indent each oiled dough rectangle lengthwise with a chopstick, stretch and slap against the counter into long wide ribbons, tear down the center seam, and boil 90 seconds.

5. The Finish: Pile hot noodles into the seasoned bowls, top with shredded braised pork, spinach, scallions, garlic, and chili flakes, then ladle smoking 350°F oil directly over the aromatics so they sizzle and bloom. Toss immediately!
