Ninja Creami Double Oreo

Midnight-dark black cocoa chocolate protein ice cream tasting just like an Oreo wafer, packed with mixed-in and crumbled Oreos — 299 calories and 35g protein per pint.

Prep Time

5 mins

Total Time

16 hours

Yield

1 pint (299 cal · 35g P)

INGREDIENTS

Tap to check off
Black Cocoa Base (299 cal · 28g C · 9g F · 35g P)
Mix-Ins & Topping

INSTRUCTIONS

Tap a step to mark done
Freeze, Spin & Double-Load

1. Blend the chocolate protein shake, sweetener, black cocoa powder, salt, and xanthan gum until jet-black and lump-free; freeze in a Creami pint at least 16 hours.

2. Run under hot water for 60 seconds, spin on Lite Ice Cream, and Respin until fudgy and smooth.

3. Hollow a center well with a butter knife, drop in 3/4 of the Oreos, and run the Mix-In cycle.

4. Crumble the remaining Oreos right on top before serving.
