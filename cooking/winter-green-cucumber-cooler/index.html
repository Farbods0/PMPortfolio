"Winter Green" Cucumber, Mint & Lime Cooler

Vibrant emerald-green party pitcher cocktail blending peel-on English cucumbers, fresh mint, razor-sharp lime juice, vodka, and an optional subtle jalapeño kick.

Prep

10 mins

Total

15 mins

Yield

12–16 drinks

Ingredients

Tap to check off
Batch Scale
Batched Dispenser / Pitcher

Instructions

Tap a step when done
Blend, Fine-Strain & Chill

1. Blend the chopped peel-on cucumbers, fresh mint leaves, lime juice, simple syrup, and optional seeded jalapeño on high for 20 seconds until completely liquefied.

2. Pour through a fine-mesh strainer into a large pitcher or drink dispenser, pressing the solids firmly to extract every drop of bright emerald juice.

3. Stir in the 750 ml vodka and cold water (or serve concentrated over ice topped with club soda) and chill thoroughly before guests arrive.
