About Me

Engineer by training. Builder at heart.

I’m a product management leader and technologist living in San Francisco, California. Today, I serve as Senior Director of Product Management at Visa, focusing on the Visa Analytics Platform and advocating for thoughtful technology, human centered design, and enterprise security.

I earned my MBA from Santa Clara University after graduating from San Jose State University with a B.S. in Computer Engineering. Since 2012, I've led 0 to 1 product innovation and scaled product organizations across startups and global enterprises, including Spell Train, Guidewire Software, ScreenMeet, PayPal, Swiftly, AbbVie / Allergan Data Labs, and Visa.

Outside of my day job, I’m rarely sitting still: I’m a home cooking specialist who documents scratch recipes in my personal cookbook, a part time electronic music DJ streaming live as DJ FJ, and a licensed Bay Area Realtor.

When I unplug completely, you’ll find me building custom PCs, chasing winter powder on the ski slopes, playing the piano, or mentoring young Bay Area technologists starting their careers in tech.
