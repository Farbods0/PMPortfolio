Behind the Decks

From festival floors to the booth.

My love for electronic music started well over a decade ago. When the world shut down in 2020 and live shows vanished overnight, I picked up my first controller and committed to practicing every single week — recreating that unmistakable shared energy from home.

After exploring genres across the electronic spectrum, I locked into a signature blend of warm vocal house, driving basslines, and melodic club cuts. Every set is built to capture the exact euphoria of hearing your favorite artist live.
